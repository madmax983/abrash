⚡ Bolt: [performance improvement] Elide intermediate allocations in `compute_face_normals`

💡 What: Refactored `compute_face_normals` in `mesh.rs` to use `Vec::with_capacity` and a `for` loop instead of an `Iterator::map().collect()` chain.
🎯 Why: `Iterator::map().collect()` can occasionally incur extra mapping/allocation overhead; using an explicit pre-allocation ensures the internal vector is sized exactly once before insertion.
📊 Impact: Minor speedup and more explicit control over capacity.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with `cargo bench -p abrash-core`.
