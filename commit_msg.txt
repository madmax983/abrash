⚡ Bolt: Replace reserve_exact with reserve on dynamic frame buffers

💡 What:
Replaced `.reserve_exact()` with `.reserve()` in `CpuRenderer::extract_draw_list_into` and `Scene::extract_into` for dynamically populated thread-local and passed vectors (`world_aabbs`, `draw_list.batches`, `draw_list.vertices`, `draw_list.lights`).

🎯 Why:
As noted in "Persona 'Bolt' Learning: Do not use `Vec::reserve_exact()` on dynamic buffers that are cleared and repopulated periodically", using `reserve_exact()` on buffers that may grow slightly across frames defeats standard amortized growth mechanisms and triggers continuous O(N) heap reallocations.

📊 Impact:
Significantly reduces frame-to-frame heap memory allocations when the scene dynamically shifts in size, improving CPU frame times on non-static scenes.

🔬 Measurement:
Run `cargo bench --bench scene_render`.
