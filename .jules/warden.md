**2025-04-25 - [Pixel Sort SendPtr OOB Fix]**
**Threat:** The `SendPtr` bounds check in the parallel `pixel_sort` effect incorrectly referenced the entire framebuffer length for its inner bounds check, which bypassed bounds protections when computing columns, leading to an exploitable out-of-bounds vulnerability when processing small framebuffers sequentially after a larger buffer allocation.
**Defense:** Hardened the boundaries by enforcing strict iteration bounds relative to the height instead of the framebuffer length. Fixed bounds checks on tile rendering parallel allocation calculations. Hardened pointer alignment and vector processing bounds in `pixel_sort` bounds and iterators.
